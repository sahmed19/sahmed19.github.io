---
title: "SPOOKULELE: Frame Data Editor & Visualizer"
project_id: frame-data-editor
description: "Tune attack timing, hitboxes, and on-hit effects in Unity with immediate in-game feedback."
---

* Visualized attack timing alongside animations so designers and animators could tune combat frame data.
* Editable damage, cancellation, invincibility, multi-hit, and "sticky" frames that nudge the player toward the nearest enemy.
* Live knockback and screenshake edits with immediate in-game feedback and no recompilation.
* Enemy profiling, hitbox visualization, and built-in frame stepping.
