---
title: "SPOOKULELE: Character Ubershader"
project_id: character-ubershader
description: "A shared character shader for stylized lighting, anisotropic hair, parallax eyes, and gameplay effects."
---

Created a shared shader to handle all character materials in SPOOKULELE.

* Directional subsurface scattering
* Kajiya-Kay anisotropic hair shading
* Parallax occlusion mapping for eyes
* Character effects including flashes and disintegration
